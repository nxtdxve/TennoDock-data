# Item icons (sources)

Pictures of items that Digital Extremes' Public Export lists without a picture, for every page of TennoDock that shows
such an item (#270). Today the Railjack ordnance: Tycho Seeker, Milati and Galvarc, each with MK I to MK III, share one
icon per family. File name = name of the icon file in public-export-plus (`ExportRailjackWeapons[].icon`) without its
extension; TennoDock looks the picture up by that name.

**Origin:** The icons are textures from the game files of Warframe, © Digital Extremes. browse.wf (calamity-inc) unpacks and serves them; the icon path of each item comes from calamity-inc/warframe-public-export-plus (`ExportRailjackWeapons[].icon`). The MIT licence of browse.wf and warframe-public-export-plus covers their code and data, not these images. No licence covers the images themselves, and Digital Extremes does not publish them in its Public Export (no entry in `ExportManifest`, no hash in public-export-plus `ExportImages`).

**Rights:** The images are © Digital Extremes (Warframe). Whether TennoDock may ship them in a published app is an open legal question for TennoDock's release phase (phase 9). They are used here for informative purposes only; TennoDock is not affiliated with Digital Extremes.

**Fetched** on 2026-11-15 from `https://browse.wf<icon path>` (512 × 512 PNG, 110 to 135 KB), scaled to 128 × 128 px in two steps (rows of 40 to 64 px on 2× screens), WebP quality 85.
