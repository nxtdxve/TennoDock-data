# Intrinsics (sources)

Symbols of the nine intrinsic classes (five Railjack, four Drifter) for the page Mastery in TennoDock (#96, #153).
Digital Extremes' Public Export has no pictures of the intrinsics: `ExportIntrinsics` lists only the five Railjack classes,
without a path, and no image in `ExportManifest` matches. TennoDock uses only the alpha channel, as a mask in the text colour.

**Rights:** The images are © Digital Extremes (Warframe). Whether TennoDock may ship them in a published app is an open legal question for TennoDock's release phase (phase 9). They are used here for informative purposes only; TennoDock is not affiliated with Digital Extremes. WARFRAME Wiki content is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.

**Fetched** in October 2026 (#96), scaled to 128 × 128 px with only the alpha channel kept (grey + alpha) and shipped in the
app until #153; moved here on 2026-11-06 as lossless WebP (every visible pixel the same as the app's PNG, checked
2026-11-06). File name: the class as TennoDock names it (`LPS_TACTICAL` → `tactical`, `LPS_DRIFT_COMBAT` → `drift-combat`).
