# Mod card frames (sources)

Frame parts of the mod cards in TennoDock (#114, #126). TennoDock puts them together into cards that look like the cards in
the game; the mod image itself comes from Digital Extremes' Public Export at runtime.

**Rights:** The images are © Digital Extremes (Warframe). Whether TennoDock may ship them in a published app is an open legal question for TennoDock's release phase (phase 9). They are used here for informative purposes only; TennoDock is not affiliated with Digital Extremes. The WARFRAME Wiki provides the parts on its page [Mod/Assets](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod/Assets);
wiki content is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. WFCD/genesis-assets is licensed Apache-2.0 for the collection; the pull requests
name DE's Warframe Arsenal Twitch extension and the Warframe Companion app as the origin of the parts.

## Archon (made for TennoDock)

The game has its own frame for Archon mods; neither the wiki nor genesis-assets has its parts. These three parts were made
on 2026-11-06 for TennoDock (approved by the maintainer) in the format and position of the Legendary parts, so they sit in the
standard layout of the cards.

- **Shape:** traced from the game's mod frame mask for Archon mods (top and bottom). The side light is the cross-section of
  the strut in the top part.
- **Colours and pixels:** from WARFRAME Wiki card images: the pixel-wise median of the five Archon mod cards (254 × 373,
  uploaded 2023-10-19, fetched 2026-11-06), so that the mod image behind the frame drops out: ArchonContinuityMod.png,
  ArchonFlowMod.png, ArchonIntensifyMod.png, ArchonStretchMod.png, ArchonVitalityMod.png
  (`https://wiki.warframe.com/images/<file>`).
- **Fitting:** the shape laid onto the wiki cards at scale 0.96, centred, top at y −8, bottom at y 182 (found by
  correlation); the cost capsule area taken from the mirrored left side; the ten rank lights replaced by the colour of the
  empty groove (TennoDock draws its own rank stars there); edge pixels coloured from the nearest safe inner pixel, opacity
  from the shape; placed exactly at the position of the Legendary parts (bicubic). Mean difference to the wiki cards inside
  the frame: 13.3 of 255 (without rank lights, capsule and lower tab).
- **Converted** to WebP (quality 0.9) in the browser.

## Umbra (made for TennoDock)

The Umbral and Sacrificial mods show the Legendary frame with the set emblem on top; the wiki parts of the Legendary frame
differ a little from the cards (corner lights, edges). These two parts were made on 2026-11-06 for TennoDock (approved by
the maintainer) in the format and position of the Legendary parts, so they sit in the standard layout of the cards.

- **Shape:** the opacity of the wiki parts `LegendaryFrameTop.png` and `LegendaryFrameBottom.png` (see above), laid onto the
  cards where the frame lies on them.
- **Colours and pixels:** from WARFRAME Wiki card images: the pixel-wise median of the five Umbral and Sacrificial mod cards
  (250 × 369, uploaded 2023-10-19, fetched 2026-11-06), so that the mod image behind the frame drops out:
  SacrificialPressureMod.png, SacrificialSteelMod.png, UmbralFiberMod.png, UmbralIntensifyMod.png, UmbralVitalityMod.png
  (`https://wiki.warframe.com/images/<file>`).
- **Fitting:** the cards at scale 0.96 to the parts; under the set emblem (TennoDock draws the emblem itself, from Digital
  Extremes' Public Export) and at half-transparent edges the pixels of the Legendary parts; the cost capsule area taken
  from the mirrored left side; the rank lights replaced by the colour of the empty groove (TennoDock draws its own rank
  stars there); placed exactly at the position of the Legendary parts (bicubic). Mean difference to the wiki cards inside
  the frame and the emblem: 16.7 of 255 (Legendary parts: 30.7; without rank lights, capsule and lower tab).
- **Side light:** TennoDock uses `LegendarySideLight`; it is a glow, mostly half-transparent, and cannot be taken from
  card images.
- **Converted** to WebP (quality 0.9) in the browser.
